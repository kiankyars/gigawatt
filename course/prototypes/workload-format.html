Definitions and model boundaries

The named case is one GB300 NVL72 with 72 B300 GPUs and Llama 3.1 70B. The 100-session, 40-output-token/s target is a chosen requirement, not measured rack performance. Model memory uses rounded 70 billion parameters; decimal GB and binary GiB are distinguished.

The classic Adam state account is one precision/optimizer choice. The 64 GiB KV pool is a chosen allocation. Capacity estimates omit cache sharing and block overhead; they do not establish throughput. Continuous-batching cells are decode iterations, not measured equal-duration time slots.

The H100 production power figure is source evidence. Subsequent 60-second kW traces, relative energy comparison and phase offsets are original controlled examples. Staggering explicitly requires independent jobs with unchanged service; it is not presented as deployed industry practice.
