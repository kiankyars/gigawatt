# Teaching illustration assets

Five illustrations were generated with the **built-in GPT ImageGen tool** on
2026-09-06 and copied into this repository. Prompts are preserved in
[generation-prompts.json](generation-prompts.json) and
[additional-prompts.json](additional-prompts.json). The source files are the
original generated PNGs; no manufacturer figure or paid article image was used
as a visual reference.

| Asset | Teaching role | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `campus-cutaway.png` | Locate electrical entry, computing rooms and heat rejection | Hypothetical spatial overview; no actual site topology, scale or capacity |
| `rack-anatomy.png` | Connect rack, tray and device scales | Generic equipment anatomy, not an OEM layout or piping specification |
| `cooling-cutaway.png` | Recognize cold plates, a CDU heat exchanger and outdoor rejection | Separate fluid circuits are explained in text and code; no hydraulic design |
| `power-equipment.png` | Distinguish transformer, switchgear, UPS/storage and busway functions | Equipment plate, not a single-line diagram or work procedure |
| `network-equipment.png` | Distinguish compute, switching, storage and management roles | Cable bundles do not specify a logical topology or bandwidth |

All five were visually inspected after generation. The reader supplies real
text labels and descriptions rather than relying on generated lettering.
Numerical comparisons use tested code and responsive text, so picture geometry
never establishes a rating, route, unit, quantity or result. The generation
manifest records image hashes for the current authored edition.
