## Prompt

Create a cinematic final image for a substantial technical course called “From Watts to Tokens.” It should feel like the closing frame of an exceptional engineering documentary: physically convincing, expansive, restrained, and memorable.

Format: 16:9 landscape, highest available resolution. One continuous composition, not a grid of panels or an infographic made of boxes. View a large AI data-center campus at blue hour from an elevated three-quarter angle. A carefully chosen building cutaway leads the eye from the scale of the campus into a luminous aisle of densely packed, liquid-cooled GPU racks. In the foreground, bring one compute tray into sharp focus with believable copper cold plates, coolant hoses, electrical connections and networking. Use a gradual, deliberate change of scale rather than a collage of unrelated pictures.

Make the physical dependencies legible through real equipment: a substation and electrical distribution on one side of the campus; a compact gas-turbine plant nearby; rows of outdoor air-cooled chillers with clearly visible fan banks; insulated supply and return pipes connecting the cooling plant to the building; overhead electrical busway and fiber trays inside the hall; rack manifolds and cold plates at the compute hardware. Show heat leaving the outdoor chillers as a very subtle atmospheric shimmer. There are no evaporative cooling towers in this concept. Coolant stays in pipes, not sprayed on electronics. Separate electrical cables, optical fibers and coolant pipes visibly.

The racks are the visual center of gravity. Use restrained warm light along the power infrastructure, teal accents on coolant connections and subtle violet highlights on network fibers. These colors guide attention; do not draw glowing fantasy energy beams. In one small, tasteful display at the foreground workstation, show a few short lines of generated text to connect the physical machine to an AI result. Do not turn watts into literal floating tokens or coins.

Art direction: realistic industrial materials, precise architectural perspective, elegant lighting, rich shadow detail, a restrained charcoal/steel/teal palette, exceptional depth, and a sense of the scale of human engineering. Dense technical detail should reward looking closely while the overall composition remains immediately readable from across a room. Avoid science-fiction cities, humanoid robots, brains, neon clutter, stock-photo handshakes, corporate logos, invented numerical specifications, arrows, legends, labels and explanatory paragraphs.

Leave the upper-left quarter calm and dark enough for the course title, which will be added separately in the presentation. Do not render any title or subtitle into the image. Keep critical equipment clear of the outer 5% of the frame. The result must stand on its own as a compelling closing image, not a schematic or a marketing diagram.
