# Primer background notes

The Primer's notes for all 22 slides are in the Chapter 1 section at the top of
[SPEAKER_NOTES.md](SPEAKER_NOTES.md#source-circuit-and-load--chapter-1-slide-1).
Each note sits under a hidden `<!-- speaker: primer#scene-id -->` marker, so the
Presenter window shows it beside its slide. The short cue paragraphs are what the
presenter says. Each note's `Sources:` paragraph keeps its references and the
scope details that are not spoken; the Presenter hides that paragraph. Evidence
and teaching assumptions are in [PRIMER_EVIDENCE.md](PRIMER_EVIDENCE.md).
