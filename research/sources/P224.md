## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-17: documentation “Concepts” and comparison with On-Demand Instances.

- EC2 Spot uses spare capacity. AWS may stop, terminate, or hibernate it when capacity is needed back.
- On-Demand launch requests also require available capacity; lack of capacity can prevent a launch.
- The documented reclamation behavior differentiates this Spot product from ordinary On-Demand operation.

## Evidence scope

This is AWS policy, not a definition of every bilateral GPU rental called spot. A quoted short-term rate or a market index does not establish an interruption clause. On-Demand access alone is not a reservation of future capacity.

## Teaching use

Chapter 15 should distinguish price flexibility, contract duration, and interruption rights. Ask what the offer actually promises before comparing its hourly quote with a long-term capacity commitment.
