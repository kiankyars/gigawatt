## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-17: public product page, “Inference on your terms” comparison and product FAQ.

- Serverless inference is token-billed; Dedicated Inference is GPU-hour-billed.
- Dedicated Inference assigns routing, scaling, and service lifecycle to CoreWeave while the customer selects GPU, location, and runtime.
- Inference on CKS gives the customer control of the serving stack, including runtime, scheduling, and topology, on dedicated bare-metal nodes.

## Evidence scope

The distinction is serving responsibility, not hardware ownership. Product descriptions establish available service models, not market shares, negotiated prices, or the terms of a particular AI-lab contract.

## Teaching use

Chapter 15 compares what a provider invoices and who runs serving. A laboratory can rent GPU capacity and track its own cost per token internally; the supplier need not invoice tokens. Do not equate all managed services with token billing.
