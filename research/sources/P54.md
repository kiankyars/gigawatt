## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-11 through indexed document text. Page 12 presents conceptual
architectures and allows multiple AC/DC models to coexist. Page 18 includes
MVAC-to-LVDC conversion using SSTs or other equipment. Section 4 starts the
architecture discussion; sections 8.1–8.3 address the grid and conversion interface.
This supports comparing conversion placement, not a universal adoption timeline.

## Teaching use

Trace each column to locate the AC/DC boundary. A sidecar preserves upstream AC
distribution; the illustrated direct-MV design groups upstream conversion into a
system block before 800 V DC distribution. Compare that block with a conventional
transformer plus low-voltage rectifier. Neither block count nor omitted storage,
protection and rack-conversion details establishes efficiency or a complete design.
