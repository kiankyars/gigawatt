## Research takeaway — 2026-09-06

The accessible material presents several conversion placements and an adoption
sequence. It is useful for constructing a comparison, not for declaring one
universal 800 V DC topology. Map its discussion across building distribution,
rack conversion, storage/protection and retrofit constraints.

## Cross-check and teaching decision

Compare [P05](P05.md), the earlier NVIDIA roadmap, with [P16](P16.md), the
August 2026 announcement. Keep dates attached. For D06.3 and capstone C03, draw
the architectures in a shared frame and ask which upstream AC constraints
remain when a near-rack converter is introduced.
