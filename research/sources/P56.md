## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-11. CPWR0139 explicitly documents power throttling or shutdown enabled by a liquid-cooling alert. CPWR0026 and CPWR0177 also document failed response paths. This is evidence that coordinated power reduction exists, not that every server or installation supports it.

## Teaching use

Used in the cooling-failure ending and the D10–D13 reference discussion.
The 1,000 kW heat load, 600 kW CDU capacity and 500 kW reduced heat are original
teaching assumptions. They are not product specifications, a performance ratio,
a validated control policy or a safe response deadline.
