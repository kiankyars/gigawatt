## Reviewed teaching claims

In the ideal balanced sinusoidal case, phase waveforms have equal amplitudes
and 120-degree spacing. Line-to-line voltage is the difference between two
phase voltages; its RMS magnitude is √3 times the phase-to-neutral magnitude.
The instantaneous line currents sum to zero. Each resistive branch's power
pulsates, but the three contributions sum to constant total power.

## Original worked comparison

At 100 kW total and 480 V line-to-line RMS, a balanced resistive wye load has
277.13 V phase-to-neutral RMS and 120.28 A RMS per line. Each branch averages
33.33 kW; their total stays 100 kW. All AC values above are RMS.

Three lines provide the current paths in this balanced model; zero neutral
current does not mean zero line current or zero delivered power. Draw current
arrows from their instantaneous signs, separately from load-energy arrows.
Protective earth is outside this count and is not a normal current return.
