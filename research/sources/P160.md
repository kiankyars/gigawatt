## Claim-level notes

- Schneider's **Disconnector (Isolator)** and **Switch-disconnector** sections distinguish an isolation function from a device with a specified switching duty. A plain disconnector does not establish fault-breaking capability.
- Chapter 6 uses this to compare isolation with interruption. The drawing is functional, not a switching procedure or a claim about the rating of a named installed device.
- Public page reviewed September 13, 2026. Product combinations must be judged by their individual rated duties.
