## Reviewed evidence

The sections **Mineral & Surface Estates** and **Dominance of Mineral Estate** explain severance of the two estates and the mineral owner's rights to reasonably necessary surface use. The page also discusses limitations on that use. Mineral dominance does not mean unrestricted permission to do anything on the land.

A real parcel still needs its actual chain of title, reservations, existing leases and relevant surface-use arrangements examined. The FAQ cannot establish whether a particular tract carries a conflict or which agreement resolves it.

## D12 application

Ask whether the rights obtained for the campus also address conflicting mineral-development access. Keep this Texas example separate from the hypothetical option-expiry dates, which are supplied contract assumptions rather than a rule of Texas law.
