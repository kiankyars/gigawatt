- **Domains:** [D10](../../course/DOMAIN_MAP.md#d10)
- **Use:** The affinity laws tie pump pressure, flow and speed together; dynamic head losses are approximately proportional to flow squared, so doubling flow quadruples them; adjustable-speed drives suit systems dominated by friction head; trimming an impeller 2% cuts flow about 2% and head about 4%.
- **Caution:** Industrial pumping guidance, not data-center coolant loops. The affinity relations are approximations for small changes; the impeller example holds pump speed constant. The source's 8% power figure for a 2% impeller trim does not follow from the cube law it states (1 − 0.98³ ≈ 6%); do not reuse it.
