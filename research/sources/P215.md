## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 17 September 2026: “Two-Phase Immersion Cooling: How Does It Work?” The physical sequence is boiling at heat-producing components, vapor rising to a cooled condenser and liquid returning to the bath.

## Questions and claims to verify

The cooling-water side still needs heat rejection. Do not repeat claims that this eliminates every mechanical cooling component, or generalize a specific fluid chemistry/boiling point to all systems.

## Teaching use

Chapter 11: explain why the supplied single-phase circuit differs from a boiling/condensing bath. The overgeneralized “Evaporated Oil” companion figure is not used.
