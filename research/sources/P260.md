- **Publisher:** Public Utility Commission of Texas
- **Kind:** primary_reference

- **Use:** Rule effective March 1, 2026 (Project 58480). Subsection (g)(4): for load data in ERCOT's 2026 Regional Transmission Plan compliance plan, due by April 1, 2026, a large-load customer could show financial commitment in one of three ways: security of $100,000 per MW of contracted peak demand, security equal to the utility's expected cost of equipment with a lead time of at least six months and services, or a contribution in aid of construction.
- **Caution:** The three options belong to the 2026 Regional Transmission Plan compliance plan in subsection (g); subsection (c) otherwise ties forecast inclusion to an interconnection agreement under 16 TAC §25.194, which sets the interconnection study fee and security.
