## Reviewed mechanism — 2026-09-11

The “Online UPS” section explains that the inverter continuously supplies the
load in double-conversion mode. This supports zero output transfer time between
utility and battery operation. It does not provide a universal battery-current
response time or eliminate the DC-link buffering described in P31.

The teaching inference is to separate continuity at the output from internal
transients. Do not extend the double-conversion claim to every UPS operating
mode, or identify the hypothetical 15 ms capacitor budget as a UPS transfer time.
