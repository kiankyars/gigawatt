## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-17: public SEC 2025 Form 10-K, Business overview (printed page 5), risk discussion on the business model, and Note 2, Revenue.

- The business-model discussion reports more than 98% of 2025 revenue from committed contracts; Note 2 rounds the figure to 98%.
- Customers purchase capacity through take-or-pay commitments or pay-as-you-go access. Revenue policy describes payment irrespective of utilization, with initial terms generally one to six years.
- Infrastructure is financed primarily through asset-level debt supported by customer contracts, alongside corporate financing.

## Evidence scope

These are CoreWeave's disclosures for fiscal 2025, not industry-wide shares. Contracted revenue is not profit, collected cash, or guaranteed repayment; delivery and counterparty risks remain.
