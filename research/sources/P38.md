## Reviewed evidence

- **PDF page 1, Background and Natural Gas:** the Paul Lowery Road facility occupies the former Electrolux plant, uses an existing 16-inch gas main, and paid for an 8-inch tap.
- **PDF pages 1–2, Electricity:** the first 150 MW had completed specified upgrades and approvals; the next 150 MW still depended on additional work and approval in this update.
- **PDF page 2, Tulane Road:** additional gas capacity was under study at a separate site. Nearby infrastructure did not establish that requested service.

These are the utility's historical 2025 statements. Later completion, current operating capacity, gas pressure/flow and the sites' environmental condition were not established by this review.

## D12 application

Contrast reused infrastructure, a customer-funded connection and an unresolved service request. Preserve the named site and status with every claim. The 8-inch tap does not justify describing xAI as the builder of a regional gas pipeline.
