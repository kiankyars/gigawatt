## Research takeaway — 2026-09-06

NVIDIA describes three stages: a power rack intended for existing AC facilities,
a row power center, and a facility-scale DC power block. The article states
future availability for the first two and positions the last as a longer-term
architecture. It establishes NVIDIA's announcement, not measured field results.

## Teaching decision

Use this as the primary counterpoint to [SA10](SA10.md), with the earlier
[P05](P05.md) roadmap retained for date comparison. In D06 and C03, make the
learner identify what a hybrid conversion change fixes and what it leaves
constrained. Do not turn a compatibility claim into a promise that an arbitrary
existing site can accept the new load.

Check device interfaces, protection, storage, converter performance and actual
