## Claim-level notes

Reviewed September 17, 2026: structure and distribution sections, PDF pages 1–3. Distribution decks/nozzles spread water over fill. Film fill produces thin wetted surfaces; splash fill breaks water into droplets. Air-water contact enables evaporation, and cooled water collects below.

## Questions and claims to verify

The mechanism is for an open tower. A closed-circuit evaporative cooler has a different process-fluid boundary. Dated policy, treatment and savings claims are not reused.

## Teaching use

Chapter 12 wet-tower diagram and speaker explanation: show where water contacts the air, rather than imply evaporation through a sealed pipe.
