## Claim-level notes

- Page 19 and the indexed description of figure 14 discuss balancing rack PDU loads among phases. This establishes that unequal phase loading is a real installation concern.
- The six 277 V single-phase groups drawing 20 A each, and the 60 A per-phase limit, are an original teaching example. None is represented as an Equinix service rating.
- Two groups per phase give 40/40/40 A; assigning four groups to L1 gives 80/20/20 A while retaining the same six loads. Harmonic neutral current is outside this first model.
