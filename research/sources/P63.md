## Claim-level notes

The overview distinguishes batch inference from server-side request batching. Under “Use batch inference for massive datasets,” scheduled work can tolerate hours or days. Under “Tune your workload batch size,” the guide connects workload chunks, concurrency and job deadlines. These mechanisms support a hypothetical independent evaluation queue; the source does not quantify its MW demand.

## Teaching use

D01 holds live inference and fixed support at 4 MW. The same 48 MWh queue runs at 4 MW for 12 hours or 2 MW for 24 hours, giving peaks of 8 and 6 MW. Queue availability, spare compute, unchanged energy and results, and a 24-hour deadline are explicit assumptions. Changing concurrent jobs is not the same operation as changing per-request token batching.
