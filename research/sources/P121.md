## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-12: NTT DATA's Vienna 1 fact sheet, copyright 2024. Page 2 lists 99.999% power uptime availability beneath “Service level agreement.” The contractual measurement period, exclusions and remedies are not supplied in this sheet, and no observed annual result is reported.

Page 1 describes a 15.4 MW maximum client IT load, separate A/B UPS systems with 2N redundancy, and N+1 diesel emergency generation. Page 3 identifies the location as Computerstrasse 4, 1100 Vienna, Austria.

## Teaching use

The named five-nines example is a **power SLA**. It is not application uptime, a generic mathematical prediction from 2N, or evidence that Vienna 1 completed a year with 5.256 minutes of downtime. Compare its evidence type with the Fairwater Atlanta design claim and the Crusoe Cloud operating claim.
