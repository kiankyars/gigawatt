The opening explanation places DC-link capacitors between rectifier and inverter,
with roles in bus-voltage stability, ripple and rapid load transitions. UPS is
listed among the applications. This supports including capacitors in the online
UPS explanation; it does not specify the capacitance of the pictured product.

energy buys 15 ms only when capacitance supplies the entire constant bus load.
