## Teaching decision — 2026-09-06

Use the component hierarchy to distinguish load from supply capability. Adding
power-shelf nameplates does not establish rack demand. Keep switched NVLink
connectivity distinct from literal direct wiring, and resolve the unclear
aggregation boundary in the bandwidth recommendations before using those
numbers. The course uses original synthetic power and network examples.
