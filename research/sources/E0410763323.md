## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-10: Distributed Energy Resources and Islands and Microgrids.
A local energy resource and a coordinated system capable of operating apart
from the wider grid are different propositions. Some solar installations
lose supply during a grid outage; their location alone does not prove continuity.
The page does not establish the transfer performance of a data-center system.

## Teaching use

[D03](../../course/index.html#d03-service-and-siting) changes the original 2 MW
grid contribution into a storage requirement only after explicitly assuming a
supported island. Its 4 MWh / 2 MW = 2 hour budget excludes startup and transfer.
