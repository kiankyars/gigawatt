## Claim-level notes

The grid links producers and customers through substations, transformers and lines. High-voltage transmission serves longer distances; transformers adapt voltage between parts of the network. The path shown in the opening is a simplified route within that network.

## Teaching use

Locate step-up transformation, transmission and receiving substations before entering the campus. Do not imply a dedicated plant supplies every depicted electron, or prescribe residential supply stages for a large campus.
