## Reviewed evidence

The searchable fuel-supply/technology-description paragraph states that a fuel-gas compressor is needed when site supply pressure is insufficient for the turbine. This supports distinguishing fuel availability from fuel delivered at the required pressure.

The particular turbine's pressure and quality specification, contracted gas volume, transportation terms, compression auxiliaries and startup supply remain equipment- and site-specific. No pressure threshold or compressor power was assigned to either teaching parcel from the excerpt.
