## Reviewed evidence

The introductory transcript, before Ken Ahmann's presentation, connects the land deal to power access and infrastructure investment, then discusses water/cooling choices, local construction and noise impacts, transmission and fiber. These are distinct feasibility questions; the presence of one service does not resolve the others.

The Tribal-development context informs who negotiates and benefits from a project. It does not establish a universal contract structure, parcel-size threshold, resource demand or delivery time.
