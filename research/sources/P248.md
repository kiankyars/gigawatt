- **Use:** Two 24,000-US-gallon tanks of 42°F water supplement a 55°F chilled-water supply and are sized to cool the data center for seven minutes beyond the five minutes of full-load UPS battery life. In a late-2006 outage lasting several hours, the stored water kept cooling while chilled-water pumps and air-handler fans ran on UPS and generator power.
- **Caution:** Company white paper; it does not name the facility. Servers ran for more than 15 minutes in the outage because the load was light, so that duration is not the design figure. Read on 2026-09-26 from the PDF text.
