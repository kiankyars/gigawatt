## Reviewed claim — 2026-09-11

Figure 1 of the application report shows a buck power stage built around a switch,
diode, inductor and capacitors. This non-isolated DC/DC topology does not need a
transformer. The inductor-current discussion also makes finite current change
explicit; a controlled power stage is not an instantaneous source.

## Teaching use

D04 distinguishes the complete converter circuit from a transformer component.
D05 uses that distinction when discussing battery interfaces. This low-voltage
calculation guide supplies the mechanism, not a design for the 800 V bus or a
battery-ramp timing specification.
