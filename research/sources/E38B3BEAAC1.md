## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-10: the BTM/FTM definition and diagram on printed pages 2–3.
The relevant distinction is the resource's electrical position relative to the
customer's utility meter. It does not establish island operation or a site's
allowed export. The source discusses storage; the lesson explicitly applies
that meter-boundary distinction to a hypothetical generator and storage bus.
