## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-11. Selected clock-event, temperature and power-reading definitions distinguish a configured electrical power ceiling from local temperature protection. Hardware support and the scope of a cap vary; no CDU-fault response time is established.

## Teaching use

Used in the cooling-failure ending and the D10–D13 reference discussion.
The 1,000 kW heat load, 600 kW CDU capacity and 500 kW reduced heat are original
teaching assumptions. They are not product specifications, a performance ratio,
a validated control policy or a safe response deadline.
