## Claim-level notes

Reviewed September 17, 2026: revision 1.0, printed pages 4–10, scope, selection KPIs, dimensions and performance requirements. The specification defines a shared coolant-connector mating interface together with operating and fluid-loss requirements. The OCP cold-plate workstream also lists subsequent UQD work; this note makes no latest-revision claim.

## Questions and claims to verify

Match the chosen size and supplier qualification to the actual flow, pressure, temperature and coolant. Physical mating does not establish an adequate pressure-flow operating point or qualify the full rack.

## Teaching use

Chapter 13: OCP turns a supplier boundary into a shared interface specification. Use the rack-change example to show why an interchangeable connector still needs enough hydraulic capacity.
