## Checked claim and teaching use — 2026-09-11

The FAQ distinguishes air-natural cooling from an added forced-air rating. D04 uses this to correct the assumption that every transformer needs fans, while avoiding the opposite claim that transformers never use fans. Controls and cooling accessories are separate from the core-and-windings mechanism.
