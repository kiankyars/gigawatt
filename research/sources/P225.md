## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-17: public “Detailed Pricing History,” contract-term controls, and pricing footnote.

| H100 one-year contract observation | USD per GPU-hour |
| --- | --- |
| October 2025 | 1.45–1.95 |
| January 2026 | 1.50–2.05 |
| April 2026 | 2.10–2.70 |

The table identifies ranges as the 25th–75th percentiles and prepayment as 25% for terms of three months or longer. One-, three-, and five-year controls exist, but the latter two have no public numerical quotes in the reviewed table.

## Evidence scope

These are dated contract ranges, not September quotes or the separately calculated hourly composite index. Do not fill blank tenors, interpret percentiles as minimum/maximum, or assume the longest contract always has the lowest price.

## Teaching use

Demonstrate renewal-price exposure with observed H100 rates. Three displayed observations establish an increase; showing a reversal requires including the earlier declining series as well.
