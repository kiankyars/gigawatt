## Verified correction — 2026-09-06

The public water-cooled-chiller paragraph defines COP with its ratio inverted.
Cooling COP is cooling delivered divided by work input in matching units; see
[P10](P10.md). Treat this as a specific checked correction, not a verdict on the
entire report. Other engineering claims require their own checks.

## Teaching decision

Use this source to discover architecture comparisons in D10 and D11. Build the
energy balance independently, with heat captured, compressor input and heat
rejected drawn at separate boundaries. A learner should be able to reject the
inverted ratio before using any reported equipment example.
