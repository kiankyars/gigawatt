## Reviewed definition — 2026-09-11

The opening definition and real-estate paragraph distinguish an option's right
to purchase from an obligation to purchase. The option has agreed terms and an
expiry; the definition notes consideration and required exercise conditions.

## Teaching use

D12 introduces the term before comparing a month-18 option expiry with a
month-30 service date. Investigation access and extensions remain separate
contract terms. The course does not supply a contract or assess enforceability.
