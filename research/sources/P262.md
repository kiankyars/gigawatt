- **Use:** Section 2.2 derives the Young/Daly period W = √(2μC) for platform MTBF μ and checkpoint cost C, cites Young (CACM 17(9), 1974) and Daly (FGCS 22(3), 2006), and shows that failure-free and failure-induced waste are equal at that period.
- **Caution:** First-order approximation. It assumes independent, exponentially distributed failures and a period much longer than checkpoint, downtime and recovery times; it is not a measured policy for AI training clusters.
