## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-12: the March 24, 2026 release reports 99.2% operational availability for the 12 MW / 63 MWh Redwood microgrid over seven months after its June 2025 launch. It separately says Crusoe Cloud maintains 99.9% using grid backup. The Cloud figure has no separately defined observation window in the release.

The named deployment is Crusoe Spark at Redwood Materials' campus in Sparks, Nevada. The microgrid and the Cloud service are different measurement boundaries. Neither percentage is a solar-energy share or an Abilene availability figure.

## Teaching use

The three-nines availability card identifies an **operator Cloud-service claim**. Compare this with the Fairwater Atlanta design claim (P120) and Vienna 1 power SLA (P121); do not rank the three as measured annual site reliability. The announced expansion from four to 24 Spark units is not evidence of completed capacity.
