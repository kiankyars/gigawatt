# P10 — Incorporate Minimum Efficiency Requirements for Heating and Cooling Products into Federal Acquisition Documents

- **Url:** [Original source](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/incorporate-minimum-efficiency-requirements-heating-and-cooling-products-federal)
- **Publisher:** U.S. Department of Energy, Federal Energy Management Program
- **Kind:** guidance
- **Review status:** page_reviewed
- **Reviewed on:** 2026-09-06
- **Domains:** [D01](../../course/DOMAIN_MAP.md#d01), [D10](../../course/DOMAIN_MAP.md#d10), [D11](../../course/DOMAIN_MAP.md#d11), [D15](../../course/DOMAIN_MAP.md#d15)
- **Use:** Verify the cooling COP definition: cooling effect divided by net work input in consistent units; distinguish it from reciprocal kW-per-ton ratings.
- **Caution:** Used for the explicit metric definition, including the COP footnote, rather than to prescribe current procurement thresholds or a whole-facility COP. Keep equipment and facility accounting boundaries separate.
