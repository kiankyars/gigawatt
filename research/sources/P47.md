- **Use:** Tower range compares entering and leaving water; tower approach compares leaving water with entering-air wet bulb. Performance depends on the stated heat load, flow and air conditions; closed-circuit towers separate process liquid from spray water. The cooling-tower theory section splits the heat the air absorbs into a sensible part and a latent part, and the split changes with entering dry-bulb temperature at the same wet bulb.
- **Caution:** Selected mechanism, terminology, closed-circuit and performance-curve sections reviewed. No handbook example approach, capacity or water-saving percentage is adopted as a universal design value.
