## Reviewed evidence

The environmental-assessment examples distinguish Phase I historical investigation and site inspection from Phase II sampling of contaminant types, concentrations and affected areas. Subsequent investigation and cleanup alternatives depend on the intended redevelopment.

An existing industrial building and utility connections do not answer those environmental questions. Conversely, potential contamination does not automatically make reuse impossible: the investigation, cleanup requirements and residual restrictions determine what use is feasible.
