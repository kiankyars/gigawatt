## Reviewed claim — 2026-09-11

The reference-design overview describes an isolated converter with a primary
half-bridge, transformer and secondary rectification. The transformer transfers
energy through a changing magnetic field; switching and rectification let the
whole circuit accept and deliver DC.

## Teaching use

Pair this topology with the transformer-free buck example in [P33](P33.md).
“DC/DC converter” describes the circuit's input and output; it does not name one
mandatory component. No power rating, efficiency or physical size from this
low-power example is adopted for a data-center UPS.
