## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-11. Sections 3.7–3.8, Appendix A and the service-level framework were inspected for redundancy, branch flow and failure boundaries. Spare cooling capacity matters only with compatible electrical, liquid and control paths. No universal thermal ride-through time is adopted.

Used in the cooling-failure ending and the D10–D13 reference discussion.

teaching assumptions. They are not product specifications, a performance ratio,
