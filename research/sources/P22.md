## Teaching use

Figure 1 and Sections II–IV distinguish conventional transformer-plus-rectifier paths from SST paths to low-voltage DC. Section IV describes input-series/output-parallel conversion cells and a 13.2 kV example using 1,200 V SiC devices. Teach topology and voltage sharing; leave the paper’s modeled efficiency/density comparisons in their original 2022 conditions.
