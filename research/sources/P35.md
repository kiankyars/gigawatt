## Reviewed evidence

The introduction's **Limitations of Soil Surveys** section explains that mapped areas can contain smaller conditions below the survey's resolution. Sampling is incomplete and information may be extrapolated; the survey also does not test for toxic spills. A favorable regional soil map therefore cannot establish either a foundation design or a clean parcel.

The unresolved evidence is site-specific: subsurface investigation, groundwater conditions, civil grading/drainage and separate environmental assessment. No allowable bearing pressure, foundation type or buildable acreage was derived from this report.

## D12 application

Treat soil mapping as a screen that directs further investigation. The example's remaining acreage assumes a suitable contiguous campus envelope; the arithmetic does not certify that assumption.
