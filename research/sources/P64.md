## Claim-level notes

The Power Shelves description identifies AC input and nominal 50–51 V DC output into the rack busbar. Installed redundant shelf ratings are output capabilities; they do not measure rack draw. The combined GB200/GB300 guide contains an approximate consumption example whose scope is insufficient to replace the explicitly named GB300 requirement in the enterprise reference architecture.

## Teaching use

Show AC supply → power shelves → DC busbar → compute and switch trays. Pair the 142 kW requirement with its own source and avoid a fabricated internal-loss allocation.
