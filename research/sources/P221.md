## Claim-level notes

Reviewed 2026-09-17: public product page, section “Nix the hypervisor layer.”

- CoreWeave describes running Kubernetes directly on bare-metal servers.
- It offers dedicated hardware resources while retaining cloud provisioning and observability services.

## Evidence scope

This supports the distinction between physical execution architecture and who operates the software. It does not establish that every bare-metal product includes managed Kubernetes, or that customers own the servers. Promotional performance and reliability comparisons were not treated as independent benchmarks.

## Teaching use

Chapter 15's service comparison should separate direct hardware access from managed serving responsibilities. Bare metal and managed software can coexist; they are not opposing ends of one product category.
