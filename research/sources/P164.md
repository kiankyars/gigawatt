## Claim-level notes

- Schneider's **Secondary Voltage** entry explains the wye notation used here: 480 V phase-to-phase and 277 V phase-to-neutral.
- The first number is not the voltage to earth. N means neutral; PE means protective earth. The diagram identifies conductor functions without prescribing conductor sizes or a grounding installation.
