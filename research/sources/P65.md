## Claim-level notes

Mechanical generators convert shaft motion into electricity. Thermal plants, wind and hydro can provide that motion; photovoltaic cells use a different conversion mechanism. Storage returns energy supplied earlier. This introductory distinction does not rank generation choices or use the page's dated market shares.

## Teaching use

Show the mechanical and photovoltaic routes separately before connecting both to a shared grid.
