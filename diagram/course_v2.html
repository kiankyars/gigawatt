How much capacity becomes useful compute?

Power at a rack position is not board input, processor-rail power, active silicon, or useful workload output.

05ComputeConvert facility power into useful workWhere does delivery actually bind?

Ask whether rack and board conversion, installed accelerators, workload activity, and measured operating demand turn facility power into useful work.

Who owns the handoff and its risk?

Separate facilities power, rack hardware, platform operation, and workload ownership before assigning output or utilization risk.

How much capacity becomes useful compute?

An estimate requires measured electrical inputs, installed hardware, workload configuration, and observed throughput or utilization on the same basis.

Separate IT cooling loop, facility loop, air-side cooling, heat-rejection plant, and water-account responsibility.

How much capacity becomes useful compute?

Cooling capacity and design posture constrain availability but do not quantify useful compute; use measured operating power, thermal state, and output.
