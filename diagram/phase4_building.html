Static condition · no clearing sequence

Path A unavailable

The visual does not specify a breaker operation, fault location, clearing sequence, repair action, or transfer behavior.

Path B remains

Remaining does not mean adequate, independent, correctly protected, or healthy. Those conditions require a specific design and operating state.

Rack-position result

Show the rack position as still supplied only when the load supports the surviving feed and the remaining end-to-end path can carry it. Otherwise the outcome is unresolved, not automatically continuous.

Planned path removal

Planned removal without affecting operations is a maintainability objective. It does not by itself establish tolerance of an unexpected equipment failure or operator error.

Individual fault or path interruption

Sustaining operations through an individual equipment failure or path interruption is a stronger and separately verified performance claim.
